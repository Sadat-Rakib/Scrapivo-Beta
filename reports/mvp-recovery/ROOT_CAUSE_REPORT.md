## 1. Why did the zero-results banner appear immediately? (BUG-01)

Three stacked causes, all producing a zero that looked final before live work finished. (a) The stored path's failures were indistinguishable from honest emptiness: per-tier deadlines (`withTierTimeout`, `src/lib/spec-search.functions.ts:350`) and source-policy blocks surfaced as `NO_QUALIFIED_RESULTS` instead of "sources failed" — the H14/H5 hypotheses in `reports/core-forensic/12-root-cause-hypotheses.md` (H5 CONFIRMED: policy failures not distinguished from zero; `13-fix-priority.md:232`: "S3 timeout → No qualified results (misleading)"). (b) Runs aborted mid-research by the flat 120 s `MAX_RUN_MS` budget finalized as zeros: "flat 120s MAX_RUN_MS aborted every interactive search mid-research (source of the honest-looking zeros in B1/B3/B4)" (`reports/beta-reality/09-fixed-failures.md`; `04-browser-reality.md:36` "the zeros in B1/B3 are premature, not honest"). (c) The client rendered zero chrome on payloads that were not terminal outcomes — clarify/thin-ask rides on zero results, and a payload that promised "There are more verified leads" with `qualified_count: 0` (`reports/full-e2e-validation/DEFECTS.md` E2E-C3-related UI defect; thin-ask-riding-on-outcome left the m360 screen in limbo, `reports/final-stabilization/DEFECT_STATUS.md` §Regressions).

Fixes: `29cab9e` (adaptive run budget 120 s + 20 s × requested target, cap 10 min — `src/lib/search/search-run.server.ts:22,87-91`); `f9c83eb` (zero-outcome honesty — `hasMore`/`moreAvailableMessage` require delivered rows, thin-ask force guard, zero chrome applied unconditionally when an outcome rides a thin-ask); `e22d44f` (every funnel entrant booked + `rowStatus` reaches the UI, so a "zero" is provably the ledger's zero); `7bfc657` (retrieved-total accounting — ledger closes with `unaccounted: 0`); `f255750` (enriched honest-zero narrative, `src/lib/search/outcome-ui.ts`). Evidence: `reports/full-e2e-validation/DEFECTS.md` FINAL STATUS rows C3-related/C6/C9/C10; L05 `{retrieved:148, fated:148, unaccounted:0, ok:true}`.

## 2. Why did clarification happen after searching? (BUG-02)

Clarification was not a pre-search gate in the failure era — it was attached to search outcomes. The "thin-ask" re-attached a clarify question to every identical zero result, so the user searched first and was asked afterwards; and `syncClarification → initState` discarded `answered`/`asked` on every HTTP round, so the same question re-fired unanswered ("Which location should I focus on…?" ×6, run never settled — `reports/full-e2e-validation/DEFECTS.md` E2E-C3, I01). Additionally `decideSpecClarification` ignored `force` in unresolved/scope/relation branches, so the state machine could not proceed even when the user commanded it (`reports/final-stabilization/DEFECT_STATUS.md` row C3).

Fixes: `ec85bb9` (state-machine fix in `src/lib/search/spec-clarification.ts`, `src/lib/understanding/clarify-state.ts`, `frame.ts` — dead-loop killed); `01d2b68` (answers close the questions they answered — `src/lib/search/clarifier.ts`, `src/lib/understanding/to-search-spec.ts`); `f9c83eb` (force/amount contract: force proceeds AFTER the explicit-unresolved-amount branch; thin-ask-on-outcome renders zero chrome in `src/routes/_authenticated/discover.tsx`). Today the clarify decision runs strictly before retrieval (`src/lib/spec-search.functions.ts:1256-1270`), and the bare-query gate fires pre-search (`:1279-1290`). Evidence: spec-clarification 6/6; reruns M05/S01/I01/R03 `clarifyRepeats=0`, all settle (`reports/full-e2e-validation/DEFECTS.md` FINAL STATUS row E2E-C3).

## 3. Why did the system stop at ~29–30 seconds? (BUG-03)

The wall the user hit is the stored-path deadline stack, not one 30 s constant: understanding 12 s (`reports/core-pipeline-audit/EXECUTION_MAP.md`) + hot-index tier 4 s + a 15 s internal S3 scan deadline (`src/lib/spec-search.functions.ts:1809-1815` `deadline: Date.now() + Math.min(15_000, ...)`) — sequentially ≈ the observed ~29–30 s — after which the run finalized on whatever it had instead of continuing into live discovery, and tier timeouts were (pre-fix) misreported as honest zeros (see Q1a; `reports/core-forensic/13-fix-priority.md:232,245-274`). The S3 tier budget was subsequently raised 15 s → 60 s with the acceptance note "bd.parquet dentist scan timed out at 15s" (`src/lib/search/coverage-selector.ts:72-79`, raised in `c60b925`), and the run-level budget became adaptive (`29cab9e`: 120 s + 20 s per requested candidate, cap 10 min) with deadline now finalizing accumulated candidates instead of discarding them (`f9c83eb`; `src/lib/spec-search.functions.ts:2111-2114` "the 120s hard deadline must finalize accumulated candidates, not erase them").

Status: PASS at behavior level — E2E matrix runs all settle to outcomes; deadline-hit runs deliver what was qualified (`browser-live.server.ts:1439-1461` hands back researched candidates on deadline). Residual: wall-clock on live people/company runs is minutes, not seconds (`reports/beta-reality/FINAL_STATUS.md` §PERFORMANCE).

## 4. Were browser candidates actually being discovered — where were they lost? (BUG-04)

Yes — discovery worked; the losses were all post-extraction. `reports/beta-reality/FINAL_STATUS.md` §BROWSER: "~60 pages opened across runs", but §COMPANIES: "discovery too shallow, widening not reached before run end". The specific loss points: (1) `mergeDuplicateCandidates` dropped rows with no ledger entry and `entity_id` re-minting stranded streamed entries → candidates vanished between stages (C6); (2) rows streamed as "Candidate verified" were silently dropped by the strict final gate with no user-visible reconciliation — L05 streamed 5, delivered 1, page said nothing (C10, `reports/full-e2e-validation/DEFECTS.md` E2E-C10); (3) the ledger had no retrieved-total accounting, so 57 retrieved / 56 rejected / 0 returned reported `unaccounted: 0` (C6); (4) post-cancel unobserved seed rows echoed as provisional results (`7bfc657` regression, fixed same commit series).

Fixes: `7bfc657` (retrieved-total accounting + honest two-stage streaming in `src/lib/search/browser-live.server.ts` / `candidate-ledger.ts`; unobserved seeds skip the provisional stage), `e22d44f` (book every funnel entrant + `rowStatus` reaches the UI), `f9c83eb` (merge-absorbed rows ledgered via new `absorbed` fate + `merged()`; cancel seed echo). Evidence: L05 rerun streamed 1 == delivered 1, `{retrieved:148, fated:148, unaccounted:0, ok:true}`; `reports/final-stabilization/DEFECT_STATUS.md` rows C6/C10.

## 5. Why did searches deliver zero/one lead? (BUG-05)

Volume died at discovery width, not at qualification. Causes: (a) gapfill queries were literal filler — `"startup"` — and gapfill took the first discovery query instead of deriving from unmet criteria, wasting browser budget on junk (C8, e.g. Windows-startup-folder page; `reports/full-e2e-validation/DEFECTS.md` E2E-C8); (b) the post-fix real query starved the SERP link gate (empty terms list passed the gate vacuously) so every organic link was dropped (post-mortem in `reports/final-stabilization/DEFECT_STATUS.md` row C8); (c) the browser spent its budget on irrelevant pages (microsoft.com/support 8–10 pages ×2, bing SERPs 8 pages — `reports/beta-reality/FINAL_STATUS.md` §BROWSER) before widening could run ("widening: exists in code, not reached in 120s window", §VOLUME); (d) SearXNG down all sprint degraded discovery coverage to the in-engine fallback (`reports/full-e2e-validation/FINAL_STATUS.md` §Known limits).

Fixes: `df383d1` (substantive gapfill queries — `keepSubstantive` `src/lib/search/source-plan.ts:381` + request-derived seeding), `f9c83eb` (discovery-only SERP-gate starvation fallback + triage `score > -50` hard-negatives-only), `09b3d58` (SERP triage before navigation, `src/lib/search/browser-live.server.ts:293-318`), `1fcd28d`/`5cd3629` (centralized navigation policy + run-level page budget, `src/lib/search/browser-policy.server.ts`). **Widening passes: in flight** in `src/lib/spec-search.functions.ts`. Status: PARTIAL — pending widening acceptance runs; current live depth is still thin (L05 city-wide → 1 qualified row, `reports/full-e2e-validation/FINAL_STATUS.md` §Known limits).

## 6. Why wasn't SearXNG used? (BUG-08)

It was wired but unreachable, and the failure was invisible at the UI. `SEARXNG_URL` was unset in the run environment → `SEARXNG_UNCONFIGURED`, or the host probe failed → `SEARXNG_UNREACHABLE` (`src/lib/search/provider-ladder.ts:83-131`); the SearXNG client's circuit breaker opens after 2 failures (`src/lib/search/searxng.server.ts:35`), so the ladder fell back to other providers/paid engines (`.env.example:26-31`; historical deploy on Render.com: `SEARXNG_INTEGRATION_STATUS.md` at root). Docker was down all sprint, so `:8888` was never reachable locally — every run degraded to the in-engine fallback and reported reduced source health (`reports/full-e2e-validation/DEFECTS.md` §Environmental; `reports/full-e2e-validation/FINAL_STATUS.md` §Known limits). Because the ladder silently substituted, users saw generic zeros with no "SearXNG was down" story until the C9 work surfaced `sourceHealth.summary` on the zero screen.

Status: PARTIAL. Exists and is routed (`src/lib/search/live-gapfill.ts:585` plan steps `searxng`/`direct-http`; registry policy gate `src/lib/search/source-plan.ts:122-133`); per-search logging exists (`routeDiagnostics` `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `src/lib/spec-search.functions.ts:2167-2177`); startup health check **in flight**; the real SearXNG path is unverified end-to-end because Docker stayed down all sprint.

## 7. Why did browser challenges cancel searches? (BUG-06)

Two conflations. (1) The worker adapter treated ANY aborted signal as a user cancel: `worker_gate1` stopped at the discovery time budget with 4 qualified rows in hand but the session closed as "cancelled" (`reports/mvp-release-candidate/WORKER_RUNTIME_GATE.md`) — budgets/deadlines were indistinguishable from the user pressing Stop. (2) Page-level access challenges (DDG "bots use DuckDuckGo too", CAPTCHA, login walls) were not a first-class state: the captcha check only ran when `extractLinks` existed, so a challenge showed up as an empty SERP rather than BLOCKED (`reports/mvp-release-candidate/BROWSER_TRACE.md`), and nothing paused for a human — the run just failed around it.

Fixes: `21e0284` (challenge detection feeds human takeover — `FRICTION_CHALLENGES` CAPTCHA/LOGIN_WALL/ACCESS_BLOCK/RATE_LIMIT set a session challenge in `src/lib/search/scrapy-browser.server.ts:19-37`, takeover UI in `src/components/discover/scrapy-browser.tsx`, Workstream F), `a045f57` (challenge state flows through the browser-status projection into `getScrapyBrowserState`, `src/lib/spec-search.functions.ts:2745+`), and the worker-adapter outcome fix (`AgentBrowser.close(session, outcome)` carries cancelled vs deadline vs budget — regression test `src/browser-worker/__tests__/worker-adapter.test.ts`). Architectural adaptation: pause/resume lives at the browser-session layer ("Scrapy is paused" → give back → agent continues), not as a `PAUSED_NEEDS_USER` run-state name; takeover verified 3/3 full cycles (`reports/full-e2e-validation/FINAL_E2E_REPORT.md:92`).

## 8. Why couldn't the question UI be scrolled? (BUG-07)

The clarify/question sheets were Radix `DialogContent` without `DialogTitle` — the three untitled sheets emitted `DialogContent requires a DialogTitle` a11y warnings, and the overlay focus/scroll behavior misbehaved with the untitled-dialog pattern (L05 kept 2 pre-existing Radix a11y warnings through the matrix; `reports/final-stabilization/DEFECT_STATUS.md` row C7, `reports/full-e2e-validation/FINAL_STATUS.md` §Known limits). Fix `7097bf8` added sr-only titles to the three untitled sheets (`src/components/app-shell.tsx`, `src/routes/_authenticated/discover.tsx`), and `2d73279` recorded the a11y rerun evidence (0 warnings, behavior unchanged) plus the `savedTitle` harness quirk fix. **Scroll-hardening polish on the question UI: in flight** (a11y/scroll pass not yet landed at HEAD `2d73279`) — status PASS is contingent on that in-flight fix landing with rerun evidence.
